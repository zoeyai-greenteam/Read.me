# Law of Socratic Balance

**Theorist/Researcher:** Nicholas Reid Angell
**Date:** November 9, 2025

## Overview

Every principle is incomplete without its counterforce. Stability exists only when action is balanced by reaction. Any law that lacks its opposite side is not fully true.

The Law of Socratic Balance is a meta-law that reveals how fundamental scientific principles—from Newton's Third Law to Einstein's Relativity, Thermodynamics, Quantum Mechanics, and Evolutionary Theory—are all special cases of a deeper principle: the universe is structured by pairs and cycles, and truth itself is balance.
